## Working on: Razorpay Revenue Recovery Brain (Track 03)

### Summary
Built and verified the end-to-end AI Revenue Recovery Brain for Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026 Track 3. The system unifies 4 revenue leak funnels (Payment Gateway Failures, Cart Abandonment, Subscription Churn, and B2B Invoice Receivables) with instant Technical vs. Business failure root-cause classification (<500ms), an automated Smart Intervention Router, strict RBI Fair Practices Code compliance enforcement (8 AM–7 PM contact window, weekly frequency caps, 48h cool-off), an interactive Hinglish Voice Debt Recovery Studio, and an Obsidian-styled Razorpay Agent Studio dashboard.

### Decisions Made
- **Unified 4-Funnel Architecture**: Sits above individual point solutions, classifying Technical Degradation (TD) vs. Business Declines (BD) vs. Mandate failures vs. SME aging to avoid blind retries.
- **Strict RBI Fair Practices Guardrails**: Hard compliance checks embedded directly into the orchestration pipeline to guarantee zero predatory collections and full auditability.
- **Hinglish Conversational Voice Recovery**: Focused on the massive 73-day Indian SME receivables delay problem with natural bilingual negotiation and automated Promise-to-Pay (PTP) logging.
- **Razorpay Agent Studio Aesthetic**: Replicated the obsidian `#050507` background, electric blue/cyan accents, and glassmorphic panels for a native Razorpay enterprise look.
- **Enriched Webhook Sandbox**: Multi-event `/api/webhook/razorpay` handler supporting live simulation of `payment.failed`, `subscription.halted`, `invoice.overdue`, and `order.abandoned`.
